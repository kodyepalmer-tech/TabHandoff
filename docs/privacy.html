TabHandoff Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29

TabHandoff hands a browser task to AI on your computer (local bridge / provider you configure), saves history locally, and treats ZIP export as optional sharing. No TabHandoff cloud account is required for the core path.

Summary

  • Captured: page URL/title, optional selection, optional screenshot, your instruction.
  • Processed on 127.0.0.1 and/or your chosen AI endpoint — not a TabHandoff server.
  • Retained under ~/.tabhandoff/ until you delete it.
  • ZIP / copy-brief sharing is optional and under your control.
  • We do not sell personal data or use page content for advertising.

Data the extension may access

DataPurpose
Active tab URL and titleIdentify the page
Selected textContext you chose
Visible-tab screenshotEvidence when allowed
Your instructionTask for local AI / next person
Local bridge tokenAuthenticate to loopback helper
Local AI task historyShow past results on this computer

Permissions

  • activeTab / scripting — read the page you hand off
  • contextMenus — right-click Hand off
  • storage — onboarding, bridge settings, local history cache
  • http://127.0.0.1/* — optional local bridge only

Retention & sharing

Data stays under ~/.tabhandoff/ (or TABHANDOFF_HOME) until you delete it. Uninstall does not auto-wipe that folder. Sharing happens only when you export a ZIP, copy a brief, or send files.

What we do not collect

No page cookies or passwords in packages by design. No TabHandoff cloud sync of browsing history. No advertising SDK in the extension MVP.
